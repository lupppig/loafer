# Code of conduct

Loafer contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Welcome people with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, security reports, and requests to disengage.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, trolling, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants whose behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.

Report urgent safety or conduct concerns privately to the current maintainers through the contact
information on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive reports in public
issues. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
